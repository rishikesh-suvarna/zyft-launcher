Focus treatment
A white 3 dp ring disappears on white banners and fights bright ones. Replace it with a double stroke: 2 dp ink gap (#0B0D10) + 3 dp lime ring, scale 1.06 and 8 dp elevation. The ink gap guarantees separation from any artwork; the lime guarantees separation from any background. One GradientDrawable with two strokes (or a 9-patch-free LayerDrawable) — no shader cost. The focused banner also gets its name below, so banner-only apps are still identifiable. Toggle “Focus style” in Tweaks to compare against the current treatment.
Accent · Zyft Lime #C5F36A
Hue ~95° sits far from the red (Netflix-style, YouTube) and blue (Prime-style) that dominate banners, and it isn’t white. Very high luminance gives ≥ 11:1 against the lightest end of every preset (Ocean #0A2C3B is the worst case) and 15:1 for ink text on lime fills. It reads as “signal LED”, which fits the remote-control brief. Amber #FFB547 is reserved for status only.
