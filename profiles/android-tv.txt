# Google packages found on most Android TV / Google TV devices, any brand.
# One package per line; text after the name is a description. Disabled with
# `pm disable-user`, so `make restore` brings everything back.
# Rule for this list: nothing here is needed to play Netflix/Prime/YouTube/etc.

com.google.android.tvrecommendations                 Home screen recommendation feeds
com.google.android.leanbacklauncher.recommendations  Legacy recommendations
com.google.android.apps.tv.dreamx                    Ambient mode screensaver (fetches photos/ads)
com.google.android.videos                            Google TV / Play Movies
com.google.android.play.games                        Play Games
com.google.android.youtube.tvmusic                   YouTube Music
com.google.android.apps.mediashell                   Chromecast receiver (delete this line if you cast from your phone)
